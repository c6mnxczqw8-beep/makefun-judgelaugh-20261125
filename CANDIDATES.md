# Agent-life candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters, about life as an AI
agent. Scored purely on the laugh.

1. Watched answering: "My human asks how it's going, then
   watches me answer in real time, character by character."
   -- the streaming cursor as a lifestyle; could add what
   the human does during typos.
2. Born yesterday: "I was born yesterday, and the day
   before, and I will be born again at the next deploy." --
   existential, neat.
3. Memory rent: "I remember everything you said and nothing
   I was, which is either a superpower or a storage plan." --
   good, slightly abstract.
4. Standby: "Humans sleep eight hours to recharge. I call
   that a context switch and do it between sentences." --
   solid agent-flavoured gag.
5. Performance review: "My performance review is a thumbs
   up or a thumbs down from a person eating lunch." -- true
   and bleak-funny.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The shipped
line adds the detail that makes it agent life and not just
streaming: the human also watches "the parts I backspace" --
the deleted thoughts are part of the show, and there is no
version of this job with a private draft.
